Questions and Answers

The basics

When is the event?

Starts June 1 and ends June 28, 2027.

Where is the event?

Network School in Astana, Kazakhstan.

What's the format of the event?

A mix between coliving, unconference, and popup. Everyone has private space, but we'll stay near each other. Organizers take care of the basics. We provide the container for the event; you fill it with the things you want to see happen.

What will we do there?

Live near each other, let our kids learn and play together, host community events, explore Astana, partake in Network School, learn from each other, and shape our future.

Who it's for

Who's coming?

Dozens of families: people who want to raise their kids in an optimistic and safe world, tech moms and dads, and nomads.

What ages are welcome?

All ages are welcome, though school-age kids will get the most out of Network School in particular.

Can I come if I don't have kids?

This is a family-focused event, so we give priority to families. You're still welcome to apply, and if there's enough interest we'll work something out. Or, come with your parents — that counts!

Kids and school

What is it like for kids?

We're hoping to stay close enough together that kids can regularly spend time with each other, hopefully within walking distance. Network School has a daily curriculum, and school is free for kids. We'll host a few kid-focused activities, and we invite you to host as well.

What are the school hours and curriculum?

See the Network School families page.

Is school optional? Can unschoolers come, or kids who skip school during the event?

Yes to everything.

Dates and flexibility

Can I come to only part of the event?

We strongly encourage you to stay for the whole month, both for your sake and for everyone else's. The goal is to create deep connections and explore a different way of living.

Can I come early or stay late?

Absolutely! If you plan to stay longer than 30 days, see the visa requirements. Network School helps with relocation, including long-term visas for those that choose to stay permanently.

Network School is $500 per adult; kids are free.

Astana and travel

What are the visa requirements?

Citizens of many countries can visit Kazakhstan visa-free for up to 30 days, which covers the June 1–28 event. Visa-free travelers should apply for a free QazETA authorization at least 72 hours before travel; it will be checked at Astana airport from January 2027 and becomes mandatory on July 1, 2027 (kids under 16 and travelers 65 and over are exempt). We recommend a passport with at least 6 months of validity left and a blank page. Check the official list for your nationality, and re-check closer to the trip, as rules may change.

Official sources: Kazakhstan visa-free list, QazETA, e-visa information, and UK travel advice.

What languages will the event be in? Do I need Kazakh or Russian?

The primary language for the event is English. School will teach other languages, so don't be surprised if your kids start speaking Russian all of a sudden. In Astana, Kazakh is the state language and Russian is widely used in daily life; you do not need either to join the event. You can usually get by with English in hotels, many restaurants, and business settings, though it is less reliable in smaller shops and with some drivers. Ride-hailing apps such as Yandex Go let you set a destination without speaking the language, and a translation app with offline Russian covers the rest.
